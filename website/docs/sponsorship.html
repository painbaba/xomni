Sponsorship

The waitperk and perkline plugins turn your idle agent time into income. While XOMNI works, a sponsor line runs in the statusline — and every work event pays.

Mechanics

  • Sponsor line. A rotating sponsor message rendered by perkline in the statusline while the agent is working.
  • Work events. Each discrete unit of agent work — a tool call, a task step, a completed subtask — counts as one work event.
  • Impressions. Every work event generates impressions for the sponsors in the rotation. Impressions accrue while the agent works, not while you watch — that's the waitperk guarantee.
  • Payout. Earnings accumulate locally and are settled per your payout settings.

The earnings formula

earnings per work event

earnings = min( 0.5 × P × share , 0.5 × P )

  • P — the price the sponsor pays per impression, per work event.
  • share — the sponsor's share of impressions for this event, from 0 to 1.
  • The 0.5 floor. You keep at least half of every impression, no matter the share.
  • The 0.5 cap. No single sponsor can pay you more than half of P per event — the line stays diverse and no sponsor dominates your statusline.

Worked example

Suppose a sponsor pays P = 0.10 per impression and is allocated share = 0.4 of the impressions in a work event:

payout
$ earnings = min( 0.5 × 0.10 × 0.4 , 0.5 × 0.10 )
           = min( 0.02 , 0.05 )
           = 0.02 per work event

100 work events  →  2.00
1,000 work events →  20.00

Control & privacy

  • Sponsorship is opt-in. No sponsor line until you enable it.
  • You choose the sponsor categories you'll accept; XOMNI filters the rotation.
  • Sponsor messages are rendered locally — they never see your prompts, files, or keys.
  • Disable anytime with xomni perkline off.

Fairness rules

Sponsors buy impressions, never influence. XOMNI enforces three rules: the sponsor line never interrupts agent output, impressions only accrue during real work events, and the 0.5 cap keeps any single sponsor at most half of the line. The statusline stays yours; sponsors just share the spare pixels.
