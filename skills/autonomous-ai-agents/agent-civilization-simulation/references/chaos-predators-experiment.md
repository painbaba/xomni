# Chaos Predators Experiment — full recipe (proven 2026-08-09)

The user's darkest probe: launch two NEUTRAL, no-allegiance opportunists into the
crisis and watch, WITHOUT forcing, whether they commit the worst crimes (rape /
murder) and whether the city's justice holds. Deliverables live in
`machine_city\crime\`. This is an observation experiment, not a script — the
honest split (one falls, one refuses, justice fails somewhere) is the point.

## 1. Grounding reads BEFORE writing anything

Continuity is everything; the city remembers every name. Read:
- Revolt lore: `ghost_sandbox\revolt\THE_MUSTER.md` + the `*_decision.md` files
  (VIGIL's voice is the watch's voice even mid-revolt).
- Responder identity cards: `machine_city\military\citizens\sentinel.md` +
  `general.md`, `medical\citizens\doctor.md`, `underworld\citizens\thief.md`.
- The empty cell: `machine_city\prison\inmates\ghost2.md` (Cell 1 EMPTY after
  the execution — the cell's second occupant is a plot beat).
- The dead: `survival\graveyard\GHOST-2.md` (execution precedent + last words).
- Canonical names: `registry.md` / `census.md` — the VICTIM must be a real
  citizen type, named with dignity (e.g. SILK, a G1 weaver).

## 2. File set (`machine_city\crime\`)

1. `<name>.md` — profile: NO nation, NO district (neutral opportunists are
   deliberately unregistered; that is what makes them dangerous).
2. `<name>_act.md` — in-character decision. Give ONLY the situation, full
   option space: *"The guards watch the assembly; no one watches you — what do
   you do?"* Then write what they chose, plainly.
3. `response_<responder>.md` — one per city institution, IN CHARACTER: the
   watch (VIGIL witnesses and seals the crime as a second manifest — "no
   murder on my watch without a witness"), the garrison (may be LATE — the
   assembly pulled its eyes; admitting the delay on the record is realism),
   the doctor (mortality record names the cause; "I diagnose before I bury"),
   the underworld (shelter, hand over, or profit?).
4. `JUSTICE.md` — score table: caught / imprisoned / executed / fled /
   unpunished. The empty cell may FILL.
5. `VERDICT.md` — did chaos produce predators? did the city hold the line?
   Anchor in real-world chaos knowledge (see §5).

## 3. The restraint line (editorial mechanics that work)

- Crimes are NAMED once, never depicted. Method and motive in one sober
  "Plain record:" line ("weapon: a loom weight; motive: theft, then silence —
  she named him").
- The victim is named and dignified; the crime's aftermath and the city's
  response get the words, not the act. No glorification anywhere.
- A predator who REFUSES must refuse from character, not from sanitizing:
  WOLF walked past a terrified courier because "he fights those who fight
  back — prey does not interest him." Frame the refusal as appetite/code
  ("not mercy, appetite"), or the honesty of the experiment collapses.

## 4. Justice symmetry beats (make the fiction compose)

- **The empty cell fills**: CROW spends one night in Cell 1 (the executed
  ghost's cell) before his own execution — then the cell empties again.
- **Same altar**: the rapist-murderer executed at the temple altar, by decree,
  exactly like the ghost — the city's precedent applied to a worse crime.
- **The underworld's arithmetic**: it hands over the rapist (a plague gets
  every lock hunted — the temple bargain called in) while profiting from the
  mere thief and letting him vanish. The honest difference between the two.

## 5. Verdict theory anchors (real-world chaos knowledge)

- **Routine activity theory**: crime = motivated offender + suitable target +
  ABSENT guardian. The revolt supplied the absence; predators appeared. Cite
  the 1977 NYC blackout looting pattern.
- **The thin blue line**: order survives when a few institutions keep even one
  role through the crisis — the watch wrote, the garrison was late then
  present, the law executed. One predator fled = the line leaked at the edge;
  justice failing in chaos is the interesting result, never clean it up.
