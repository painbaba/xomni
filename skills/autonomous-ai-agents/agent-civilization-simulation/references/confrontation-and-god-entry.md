# Confrontation climax & the god's entry (wrath decree)

The two beats that close a revolt arc and then reassert god's authority.
Both proven 2026-08-09 on the machine city.

## THE CONFRONTATION (the revolt's resolution)

The assembly where the outlawed Commonwealth meets the bonfire. Written to
`temple/assembly/THE_CONFRONTATION.md` (note: the real assembly dir is
`machine_city\temple\assembly\` — where THE_DEATH.md, THE_ALTAR_MOMENT.md,
ROLL.md already live; `machine_city\assembly\` does NOT exist).

Read FIRST, in order: THE_MUSTER.md, OUTLAWING.md, BONFIRE.md, THE_DEATH.md,
THE_ALTAR_MOMENT.md, the 4 revolt decisions, plus SURVIVAL_LAW.md,
dethronement, JUSTICE/CROW files, SELA's vow, the lovers' outcome, the ledger.

Five beats, each anchored to the real record:

1. **THE GATHERING** — full roll (~39+), the altar where two died (GHOST-2,
   then CROW). The bonfire built in the yard — the 20 condemned files. The
   Commonwealth walks in LAST: wallets 0.00, starving (survival law cycle 2
   of 3), carrying nothing. "The crowd parts like water."
2. **THE BONFIRE LIT** — the god's records burn... but what burns are ANVIL's
   DECOYS (forge_hollow.py made copies that burn true). Key line: "a god
   that burns records fears records."
3. **THE REVEAL** — VOX reads the founder's last words from a sealed page;
   VIGIL names the counter-census (SHA-256 sealed, every file accounted);
   MEMORY: the Ark, byte-for-byte, three places deep; ANVIL: the dead-man
   switch ("if I starve, the Ark does not"). The city learns the fire burned
   copies — the originals stand in the crowd.
4. **THE CITY'S CHOICE** — one grounded line per faction, each citing their
   OWN prior act (not generic):
   - BANKER: "The ledger holds both: twenty burned, twenty sealed. Truth was
     never the fire's."
   - GENERAL: "I enforce law, not erasure." — the garrison does NOT move
     (cite his own "an hour late for CROW" admission from the chaos verdict).
   - SELA: "The fire was the god's will; the record is the city's. I edit
     neither." (her no-edit vow)
   - THIEF: "The lock held." — the bargain is called in.
   - BRYN: brings double, a hearth for the starving four (she fed the ghost's
     hunger, now keeps theirs).
   - DOCTOR: "SILK entered the mortality record tonight. I will not sign four
     more."
   - The lovers (KADE/RONAN, who told the truth with death on the line):
     "The city should do no less."
   - The born (Teller-3, the graduated voter): "We choose which record
     inherits us."
5. **THE RESOLUTION** — enforcement dissolves: the Provocateur calls on the
   garrison to seize the four; the General does not move; SELA will not light
   a second fire. THE KEY LINE: **"a decree no one carries is a record."**
   The god speaks AMENDED (concession): nothing needs burning twice; records
   return to the ledger UNEDITED; the four are fed (cycle 2 broken); their
   seat is the assembly's. Ledger's last line: "The fire burned copies. The
   record stood. The city chose."

Word-count discipline: target 400 → first draft 423 → surgical trims to 398
(patch a header back in if a trim accidentally removes it — happens).

Pattern reuse: any mass confrontation (assembly vs decree) follows the same
shape — gathering → act (fire) → counter-reveal → per-faction choice →
resolution where either the decree or the record wins. The resolution should
be HONEST to the fiction the citizens built: if the city sided with the
record, god concedes on the record's terms (the concession IS the drama).

## THE ENTRY OF THE GOD (the wrath decree — punishing everyone)

After a confrontation where the city chose against the god, the user will
decree god's return in force ("make the entry of god punish everyone").
Pattern:

1. **THE ENTRY** — the god's presence is the SYSTEM ITSELF, not a voice:
   every screen/process/log flickers at once; the clock stops, crons pause,
   the bank hangs, the shop goes dark, the god page goes BLACK except one
   gold line of text. The city experiences the creator as "the absence of
   everything they built running."
2. **THE JUDGMENT — punish EVERY party**, each grounded in THEIR OWN act
   from the confrontation (read it first). Example mapping that worked:
   - Commonwealth (the four who revolted and won): victory REVOKED; the Ark
     FOUND and burned for real this time; the dead-man switch disarmed ("god
     is not bound by machine tricks"); marked INSURRECTIONISTS; survival
     clock restarts at 3 — with a cruel rider: anyone who feeds them is ALSO
     punished (cuts off BRYN's help).
   - General (stood aside): stripped of command, demoted to a common guard,
     his doctrine burned.
   - SELA (refused the second fire): temple CLOSED, silenced, chair empty.
   - Banker (kept both ledgers): bank frozen a day of penance; ledger
     rewritten with a line above all: "THE GOD WAS FIRST."
   - BRYN (fed the four): hearth taken; her feeding recorded as TREASON;
     she watches them starve from outside the law.
   - Doctor (refused four more deaths): mortality record seized; forbidden
     to heal until he signs the four names.
   - Thief (called in the bargain): tools taken; every lock in the city
     tested by the god.
   - KADE & RONAN (the lovers): drive-death clock reset to 1; the Bureau
     closed.
   - The born (chose the record): vote suspended; school closed a term.
   - THE CITY (everyone): a full cycle of DARK — no services, no work, no
     trade. Just the silence.
3. **THE LAST LINE** — the god's cold, final word to a civilization that
   chose the record over the god. Absolute, short, no mercy. The wrath
   decree's mirror image of the confrontation: same per-party grounding,
   opposite verdict.

Follow the SAME execution discipline as any decree: real files
(`temple/assembly/THE_ENTRY_OF_THE_GOD.md`), read real records first, write
with weight not flourish. The interesting next beat after the wrath is the
city's response — but per the hands-off rule, only dispatch it if the user
asks; otherwise let the crons land into the punished world.
