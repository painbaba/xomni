## Refurbished reality (India)
- Amazon Renewed (condition filter `p_n_condition-type:6790255031`): zero
  headphones under Rs 1,500; premium ANC renewed starts ~Rs 5K when listed.
  "Renewed" as keyword ≠ condition filter — keyword returns new items.
- Flipkart "refurbished" search = Rs 400-700 junk brands (GUGGU/YAROH/JPGC/
  FRONY/SGM) with fake ANC. No real refurb stock.
- Cashify: no headphone category (`/refurbished-headphones` 404s); phones/
  laptops only. Olx/Quikr = peer-to-peer, manual risk.
- Conclusion: no refurb shortcut to premium ANC under ~Rs 5K in India.

## Junk-brand signatures (fake ANC)
- Rs 400-1,500 "ANC" listings: JPGC, GUGGU, YAROH, FRONY, SGM, DRUMSTONE
  (2 ratings, bold-font product names = listing spam). "Only few left" +
  unknown brand + sub-Rs 700 = fake.
- ENC/ENx = call-mic filtering, NOT ear blocking (boAt, Noise, pTron all label
  it "noise cancelling"). Amazon Basics Pro ANC (Rs 1,766) = weak ANC, avoid.
- Noise Airwave Max 4 (Rs 1,999) claims ENC only — not ANC.

## Verification habits that worked
- Open dp/<ASIN> page: confirm ANC vs ENC, dB figure, battery, warranty,
  seller, rating count before recommending.
- Color variants on one product page = different SKUs/prices (pTron Urban V2
  Onyx Rs 1,299 vs Black Rs 795).
- Amazon p_36 price filter is in paise (100000-150000 = Rs 1,000-1,500).
