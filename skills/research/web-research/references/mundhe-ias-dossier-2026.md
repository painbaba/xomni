**Bombay HC litigation — the pushback (Jul 7–Aug 5, 2026)**
- Legal hook: FSS Act **s.32** requires an improvement notice + ≥14 days before suspension/cancellation in routine cases; only s.32(3) "interest of public health" allows immediate suspension with recorded reasons. Courts held the FDA overused the exception.
- **At least 9 cases** where HC benches (Mumbai/Nagpur/Aurangabad) stayed or reversed FDA suspensions over the prior month — restored licences, or converted suspensions to improvement/show-cause notices (IE, Aug 3: https://indianexpress.com/article/cities/mumbai/raids-shut-down-mumbai-restaurants-high-court-steps-in-10818440/ ).
- **Jul 7** Nagpur bench (Justice Anil S. Kilor): set aside suspension of Wardha's 80-yr-old Goras Bhandar — only the industrial-ice buttermilk was a "risk to human health"; slippery floors ≠ "exceptional circumstances".
- **Late Jul** Aurangabad bench (Justice S.G. Chapalgaonkar): stayed suspensions of ≥6 milk suppliers/vendors (synthetic milk cases); broken tiles/open drains not "serious… no exigencies."
- **Jul 29** ACJ Ravindra V. Ghuge bench restored **Park Inn by Radisson** (Navi Mumbai, 4-star) — "two insects" in a 95%-compliant kitchen can't shut a hotel; "We're in India, have to take realistic approach" (NewsGram: https://www.newsgram.com/maharashtra/2026/07/31/bombay-hc-restores-park-inn-by-radisson-license-insects ).
- **Jul 30–31 Poornima/Mantralaya episode:** court compared FDA reports with 4 court-appointed lawyers' inspection of Mantralaya/Vidhan Bhavan/Annexe canteens (refrigeration "in shambles"); HC: "Either suspend the licence of the Mantralaya canteen or restore Poornima's… We want parity… None, and especially VVIPs, are above the law… You are shooting first and then asking questions"; FDA's 30–40-min canteen inspections vs lengthy private ones deemed unreliable; FDA agreed to treat Poornima's suspension as an improvement notice. Court had ordered govt-canteen inspections with video evidence on Jul 30 (ACJ Ghuge + Justice Gautam Ankhad).
- **Aug 1** "Be fair to all": HC questioned FDA certifying Mantralaya/Vidhan Bhavan canteens "98% compliant" against photographic evidence of filth; heard Park Inn + Poornima petitions (Moneycontrol: https://www.moneycontrol.com/city/be-fair-to-all-bombay-hc-flags-tukaram-mundhe-led-fda-s-double-standards-in-action-against-private-govt-eateries-article-13990707.html ).
- **Aug 3** Pawan Bar & Restaurant (Navi Mumbai): inspected Jun 29 (63% compliance), suspended next day; Jul 14 re-inspection 100% but licence never restored. HC revoked suspension, threatened **costs**: "We will calculate the average income per day of the establishment, and that will be charged to you… Who will undo the damage caused to the petitioner?" Also: "Your problem is that the department was sleeping all these years and now it has woken up. You have a laudable Chief…" (IE: https://indianexpress.com/article/legal-news/food-safety-body-cornered-bombay-high-court-says-youll-be-charged-10818440/ ).
- **HC canteen episode (the viral twist):** day after being slammed for "selective" raids, FDA inspected **three Bombay HC canteens on Jul 31 — two suspended for lacking valid FSSAI licences**, third got an improvement notice (NewsGram, Aug 3: https://www.newsgram.com/maharashtra/2026/08/03/tukaram-mundhe-bombay-high-court-canteen-maharashtra-fda-raid ). Court also flagged that FDA's video evidence was of Poornima, not Mantralaya.
