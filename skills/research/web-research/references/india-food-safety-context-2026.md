## Adulteration scale (the headline stats)
- **2011 FSSAI National Survey on Adulteration of Milk** (released 2 Jan 2012): 1,791 samples, 33 states/UTs; **68.4% non-conforming** (46.8% deficient fat/SNF; 44.69% adulterated, mostly water/reconstitution); 147 of 1,226 non-conforming samples had **detergent** — confined to Bihar, Jharkhand, Odisha, West Bengal. BBC (Jan 2012) https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/world-asia-16487993 ; DairyReporter (12 Jan 2012) https://www.dairyreporter.com/Article/2012/01/12/Indian-detergent-scare-reveals-widespread-milk-adulteration/ ; FSSAI-hosted clipping https://fssai.gov.in/upload/media/FSSAI_News_Milk_ETRetail_18_10_2018.pdf
- **Nuance (FSSAI CEO 2018):** only ~10-12% of milk carries safety-risk adulterants; most non-conformance is water/fat-SNF manipulation (economic, not toxic). https://fssai.gov.in/upload/media/5b4d923bd4d90FSSAI_News_Milk_FinancialExpress_27_05_2018.pdf
- **2018-19 FSSAI survey:** 6,432 samples, 1,103 districts; ~37.7% failed quality norms; ~7% "unfit" (The Hindu, 18-19 Oct 2019; headlines differ — flag "37.7% vs 41% poor quality" discrepancy inline).
- **FY26:** ~1 in 6 samples failed safety/quality checks (NDTV, 5 Jun 2026). **2015 (Lok Sabha):** 14,599 of 74,010 (19.72%) failed. https://www.zeebiz.com/companies/news-is-your-food-safe-20-samples-tested-in-2015-were-adulterated-4116
- **WHO (4 Jun 2026, 2021 data):** 866M illnesses / 1.5M deaths/yr globally. https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/food-safety — India-specific mortality not centrally published.
- Milk adulterants: water, SMP, urea, detergent, starch, glucose, hydrogen peroxide, formalin (2011 list; FSSAI DART kits = the public countermeasure).

## FSSAI & enforcement
- Statutory under FSS Act 2006 (in force 2011); sets standards + central licensing; **enforcement delegated to state FDAs** (Food Safety Commissioners / Designated Officers / Food Safety Officers) — state FDAs do the raiding. SC held s.59 (adulteration) overrides IPC 272/273 (SCC Online, 26 Feb 2024).
- "Toothless" criticism: Inventiva (15 Jun 2026) "Bournvita, Patanjali, MDH, Everest: How FSSAI Is Becoming a Toothless Tiger"; Scroll.in (25 Jun 2026) — FSSAI took credit for a Bournvita 'crackdown' but gave the drink a clean chit; BBC (20 May 2026) "India has food safety laws. So why can't it guarantee safe food?"; Delhi HC struck down FSSAI animal-feed rules (LawBeat, 10 Apr 2026). Penalty anecdote: fake-paneer seller fined ₹5,000 (TOI, 17 Apr 2026). ~50% of FSS Act cases in UP alone (FACTLY, 23 Mar 2021).
- MDH/Everest ethylene-oxide scare (Apr-May 2024; FSSAI found none in tested samples — Business Today 22 May 2024). Patanjali ads: SC refused Ramdev apology (The Hindu, 10 Apr 2024).

## Dairy economics
- India: **~239 MT milk (2023-24), +63% in a decade**, world's largest producer (PIB/News on Air, 29 Sep 2025).
- **~80% of milk flows through unorganized channels** (loose milk/unregistered dairies — where adulteration concentrates; industry analyses: ClearIAS 9 Jul 2026, IBEF 19 Jan 2021). Amul/GCMMF: brand ₹90,000 cr FY25 (ET Retail, 3 Apr 2025) → **₹1 lakh crore FY26** (Moneycontrol, 5 Apr 2026).
- Adulteration persists because water/fat-SNF faking cuts cost at scale in a fragmented supply chain; Mundhe: milk/paneer supply *fell* once enforcement began (India Today, 14 Jul 2026) — adulterated product exiting the market.

## Restaurant hygiene
- Delhi surveillance drives 2015-2017 sampled McDonald's, Pizza Hut, Domino's, KFC (ET Retail Aug 2015 https://retail.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/food-entertainment/food-services/food-dept-collects-samples-from-mcdonalds-pizza-hut/47982276 ; India Today 16 Jan 2017). May 2015: Delhi street food showed high faecal coliform (India Today, 29 May 2015). 2025: 36 samples from 12 chains (KFC, McDonald's, Subway, Pizza Hut, Barista, Haldiram, Burger King) failed (ZeeBiz, 18 May 2025).
- Hygiene ratings: FSSAI voluntary scheme, low uptake (Lucknow lagging — TOI 20 Jul 2026); MCD Delhi A-to-D NYC-style ratings (Aug 2026). Understaffing: "3 inspectors for 50,000 restaurants" (syndicated, Mar 2017). Licence fees: tiered, nominal for petty businesses; **exact current figures UNVERIFIED — check FoSCoS** https://foscos.fssai.gov.in/
