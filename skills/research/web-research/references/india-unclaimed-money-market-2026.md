## Official portals (free, self-serve — the baseline any startup competes against)
- RBI **UDGAM**: udgam.rbi.org.in — search unclaimed deposits across banks (30+ banks; all since Sep 28, 2023).
- IRDAI **Bima Bharosa**: bimabharosa.irdai.gov.in/Home/UnclaimedAmountsQuery — unclaimed amounts across all life/general/health insurers by name/DOB/PAN/Aadhaar.
- DFS **Unclaimed Assets Portal**: unclaimedassetsportal.in — one gateway across banks (UDGAM), insurance, EPFO, IEPFA.
- **IEPFA**: iepfa.gov.in / iepf.gov.in — unclaimed shares/dividends (Form IEPF-5 claim process).
- **EPFO E-PRAAPTI** (launching Aug-end 2026): Aadhaar-based portal to trace/link/claim old inactive PF accounts.
- Unclaimed >10 yrs in insurance transfers to Senior Citizens' Welfare Fund (SCWF); claims possible up to 25 yrs from transfer; then escheat to govt. Insurers must pay unclaimed amounts WITH interest.

## Fee structures (typical/legal)
- **No statutory cap on finder/claims-assistance fees found** (no live evidence) — unregulated market, unlike US state unclaimed-property finder rules.
- EPF/IEPF consultants: flat **₹5,000–10,000** upfront OR **2–3% of corpus**; hidden costs/escalation can reach **20–40%** of savings (kustodian.life guide: kustodian.life/resources/pf-epf-consultant-charges-price — also documents bribe-based and effort-based models).
- Insurance claims assistance: **15–20% + GST success fee** (Insurance Samadhan; ₹999 registration, no-win-no-fee).
- IEPF consultants commonly advertise success-fee-only (Legacy, Expertvuw offers free IEPF check).
- Claim timeline: insurers ~30-90 days post-documents; IEPF-5 claims typically months; EPFO auto-claim pilot for Aadhaar-verified inoperative accounts ≤₹1,000.
