## Ideal video length (documentary/education focus)
- **air.io dataset (18,080 channels, 4,538,463 videos, 2025–Mar 2026, Shorts excluded):** longer beats shorter in almost every niche; 5–10 min is most common but weakest band. Business/Finance peaks at 1–2 hrs (10.79% engagement at 100K–1M tier — highest in study); at 1M–10M tier 10–20 min leads 3.21% vs 2.02% for 5–10 min (~63% engagement penalty for 7-min vs 12-min videos). Correlational, not causal — air.io/en/audience-growth/ideal-youtube-video-length-by-niche-data-from-18000-channels (updated Jun 8 2026)
- Backlinko 1.3M-video study (2017, canonical): average first-page video = 14m50s; longer videos significantly outrank short ones; exact-keyword-in-title has only weak ranking correlation — backlinko.com/youtube-ranking-factors
- True-crime guides: "20–40 minutes for optimal monetization"; deep dives 20–45 min maximize mid-rolls; avg true-crime watch time 15–30 min; 73% of consumers women 25–54 — fluxnote.io/guides/true-crime-youtube-channel-usa-guide
- **Mid-roll economics:** 8-min minimum to enable mid-rolls (pre/post-roll only below; Shorts never) — vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-8-minute-mid-roll-ads/. Revenue math: 7-min @$15 CPM ≈ $8–12 RPM → 9-min with 1 mid-roll $12–18 RPM (+40–60%) → 12–15 min with 2–3 mid-rolls $18–28 RPM (2–3×) — fluxnote.io/guides/youtube-mid-roll-ads-minimum-video-length-2026. Placement: first mid-roll ~40–50% in, ≥3–4 min apart, max ~4/video. Counterpoint: retention > length (12-min @30% retention loses to 7-min @70%) — revalense.com/posts/2025/video-length-ad-revenue/

## Retention benchmarks (2026)
- Platform: poor <30%, average ~35–45%, good 50–60%, perfect >70%. By length: <5 min → 70–90%, 5–10 min → 50–70%, 10–20 min → 40–60%, **20+ min → 30–50%** — lenostube.com/en/youtube-audience-retention-average-good-and-best-benchmarks/
- Agency view: 8–15 min → 40–55% healthy; 20–30+ min → 30–45% healthy; **50% on >15 min = exceptional**; algorithm weights TOTAL watch time (35% of 30 min beats 60% of 3 min); restructuring openings improved post-30s retention 15–20 pts — humbleandbrag.com/blog/youtube-audience-retention-benchmarks

## Chapters
- Official rules: first timestamp 00:00, ≥3 timestamps ascending, each chapter ≥10 s; auto-chapters on by default, manual overrides — support.google.com/youtube/answer/9884579
- Performance: chaptered content = **41% higher average watch time** (5,000+ creator posts) — podprepper.com/blog/how-to-use-youtube-chapters-to-boost-watch-time-on-podcasts
- Chapters feed Google **Key Moments** — each chapter can rank independently for different queries — gyre.pro/blog/youtube-video-chapters-how-to-add-them-why-they-increase-views

## Series vs one-offs
- Series: higher subscriber conversion, playlist auto-play compounds session watch time (positive ranking signal), return-visit habit. Standalone: multiplies search entry points. Recommended hybrid "soft series" (standalone titles + playlist organization) — smmnut.com/blog/youtube-series-vs-standalone-videos-which-grows-channels-faster/
- Case evidence: top faceless doc channel runs standalone deep dives 2×/week on recurring themes, not numbered episodes — facelessindex.com/case-studies

## Monetization mechanics & revenue stack
- **Official split: 55% of net ad revenue (Watch Page); Shorts 45% of creator pool** — support.google.com/youtube/answer/72902 (Partner earnings overview). Premium revenue ∝ members' watch time on your content — youtube.com/intl/en_us/creators/earn/ads-and-premium/
- YPP entry: 1,000 subs + 4,000 public watch hrs (or 10M Shorts views/90 days); monetization removable after 6 months inactivity — support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851
- Faceless-channel case studies (income estimates from public data): finance-doc channel ~$28K/mo = AdSense ~$18K + sponsorships ~$7K + affiliates ~$2K + memberships ~$1K (1.4M subs, 2 vids/wk, 14 mo to monetize; breakout on one 180K-view video at months 4–6); horror channel ~$11.2K/mo (720K subs; background-footage switch doubled CPM $2→$6+) — facelessindex.com/case-studies
- Magnates Media (premium faceless business docs, 20–40 min): 1.83M subs, ~2M views/mo, est. $32K–$48K/mo at est. RPM $16–24 — aivideoempire.com/case-studies/magnates-media; vidIQ shows 1.86M subs / 193.28M views — vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/@magnatesmedia/
- Sponsorship rate cards (IMH/NeoReach/HypeAuditor 2025): dedicated-video rates $150–$3.4K; finance ~$525–$3.4K — vidscored.com/tools/sponsorship-rates
- Affiliates: VPNs $30–100/signup (NordVPN/ExpressVPN), Audible $5–15/trial — FluxNote true-crime guide

## Policy risk for faceless/AI channels (critical)
- **July 15, 2025:** "repetitious content" renamed **"inauthentic content"**; covers repetitive/mass-produced content; "channels where content feels interchangeable… produced using a template" ineligible for monetization; reused/scraped content = channel-level risk — support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392
- Undisclosed AI voice/likeness, synthetic-looking thumbnails/metadata → limited ads, demonetization, or removal (2024–25 rule rewrite) — influencermarketinghub.com/youtube-inauthentic-content/ (Nov 23, 2025)
- Compliance playbook: original scripts/narration/graphics, materially varied output, AI disclosure

## Packaging (titles/thumbnails)
- CTR: platform avg 4–6%, strong 8–10%, elite 12%+; by niche: Education/How-to 5–9%, Finance/Business 6–10%, Entertainment 4–8% — heistbrain.com/benchmarks/youtube-ctr.html. By source: Search 8–15%, Browse 3–7%, Suggested ~9.5% — humbleandbrag.com/blog/youtube-ctr-benchmarks
- **"Quality CTR":** YouTube evaluates the 30 s after the click, not just the click — humbleandbrag CTR benchmarks
- 3-thumbnail A/B testing: a 1% CTR gain → +20–30% lifetime views — HeistBrain
- Documentary packaging patterns: curiosity-gap + payoff titles ("The rise and fall of X"), mystery hooks ("Solved after 30 years — how DNA caught the killer"), single-focal-point high-contrast thumbnails, "lesser-known story" positioning to beat saturated topics
