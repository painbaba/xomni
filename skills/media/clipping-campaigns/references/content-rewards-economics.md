# Content Rewards — verified platform data (Aug 2026)

Live research from contentrewards.com (no referral links, direct platform access).
Everything below was read from the platform itself on 7 Aug 2026.

## Platform mechanics (from the official FAQ)
- Signup: contentrewards.com/onboarding?type=creator → "Continue with Google" (no forms).
- Payouts go through Whop → bank/UPI. Link social accounts first (public accounts only;
  private accounts rejected). Follower count does NOT matter — platform FAQ says so.
- **7% fee** taken from all clipper payouts.
- Payout cadence: every 7 days, per submission that earned money; 7-day verification
  window after submission before payout lands in Whop balance.
- Minimum payout: set per campaign (shown on its page). Max payout: caps one video's
  earnings (e.g. $200-430); after reaching it the video stops earning.
- Rejection reasons: breaks guidelines, "appears botted", inappropriate content.
- "Flagged" = unusual activity signals (spikes in views, possible botted traffic) →
  manual review. Instant ban for botting (written in campaign rules).
- Each video can only be submitted once; only NEW videos (created after joining the
  campaign) qualify; unlimited submissions per campaign.

## Blitz campaign brief structure (what a real brief contains)
Notion brief ("Blitz | VIDEO Brief #1") sections:
- Key Info: platforms (TikTok/IG/YT/X), GEO rule (60% US/UK/CA), language (English),
  formats (UGC/Faceless), official pages.
- Core principle: native skill-gaming framing ("never feel like a win-big casino pitch").
- "Every video must": name the app (on-screen hook/description/CTA), tag official
  account, campaign hashtag (#BlitzMOVE1), promo code (BT06A) in video or caption,
  trending music added IN-PLATFORM at publish time.
- DON'TS (auto-reject): gambling/casino/jackpot/"win big money" framing; "free money /
  glitch / method / guaranteed-win" language; banned topics (drugs, alcohol, gambling,
  adult, violence, religion, politics, hate, profanity); competitor bashing.
- Concepts: Streamer Reaction (split-screen), Ranking (Top-3 tier list), User's Phone
  (found footage), App Store Scroll + Reveal, UGC Hook + Gameplay.
- Every concept has two paths: Option 1 = create your own edit (they provide gameplay
  footage + UGC hooks via Dropbox folders), Option 2 = "post ready creatives" (finished
  videos, zero editing — best for new accounts).
- Resources: Dropbox folder links (dl=0), Google Docs guidelines.

Ready-creatives folder: 30 files "UGC_Ninja_Blitz #1__V<n>_C1_EN_1080x1920.mp4",
15-28s each, ~16-33MB — a month of daily posts with no editing.

## Guru-funnel evidence (why hype ≠ income)
- Unreal Ishan "₹50K in 15 days" (1 day old, 10K views): description = referral links
  (contentrewards.com/r/unreaishan) + 3 Whop course links (Clipping Capital, Clippedin,
  ClipHaus). His own proof: ~3,000 views ≈ ₹133 → ~$0.5-1 per 1K.
- RAJ GAMER64: contentrewards.com/r/rajgamer64 referral.
- Vornix "₹1 Lakh": Proton VPN affiliate, ElevenLabs affiliate, Discord "master prompt",
  IG plug — monetizes the audience, not the clips.
- Dan Kieft (1M views): Opus Pro affiliate (?via=whop), paid AI-toolkit carrd.
- Pawan Rex: WhatsApp number for his coaching group.
- Common thread: every "earn with clipping" video's description is 30-50% affiliate
  links. The sellers earn from clippers signing up through their links, not from views.

## Realistic beginner projections (be honest with users)
- CPM avg ~$1.25/1K ≈ ₹105/1K verified views.
- Beginner month 1-3: most clips flop (<1K views) or get rejected; ₹0-15K/month is the
  honest band for consistent output.
- 100 clips/day across 10 accounts does NOT fix the constraint (tier-1 audience + viral
  probability) and invites reused-content/spam flags. One account, 2-3 good clips/day,
  English US-content campaigns is the sane scale; scale volume only after clips prove
  they survive review.
- YT Shorts ad-revenue gate (different from campaign payouts): 1,000 subs + 10M Shorts
  views/90 days per account, verified from YouTube's own support page (72851). India
  Shorts RPM ₹2-6/1K. Clipping campaigns pay per-view WITHOUT the 10M gate — that's
  why campaigns beat channel monetization for a beginner.
